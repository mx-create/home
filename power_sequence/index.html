Rows are tinted by type:raillogic. Use ↑ ↓ (or Alt+arrow in a cell) to move a row. Click “Start after” or “Stop after” for a list: the rows just above and below come first. Click any cell and press Ctrl+V to paste a block from Excel; it fills from that cell and adds rows as needed. VCCINT in “Start after” waits for VCCINT to cross the PG threshold; VCCINT.start waits only for it to begin ramping. In “Stop after”, a name waits for that signal to fall below the off threshold. Times accept ms, us or s; levels accept 3V3.
