Groups are combined as complex impedances in parallel, so ESR, ESL and anti-resonance all come through. Per part: ESR = (RS + tanδ/(2πfC))·kT. kC is the capacitance left after DC bias, temperature and ageing; kT is the cold-ESR multiplier.

Ripple current divides by admittance at fSW: Ii ≈ IC,rms·|Yi/Ybank|. Check each group against its rated ripple current. A dip-then-peak in |Z| between two groups is an anti-resonance between the ceramic ESL and the bulk capacitor.

Type I, II and III compared

Each type adds one more pole–zero pair to the integrator. The extra zeros are what buy phase at crossover; the extra poles roll the gain off again before switching noise gets through.
