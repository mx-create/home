Load an IBIS file and every signal pin gets a critical length from the fastest edge of its driver. Enter or paste your routed trace lengths to see which nets have become transmission lines and need controlled impedance and termination. A SPICE waveform export works too.

Critical length check by pin

Each pin takes the fastest edge of its driver model. That sets its max frequency, f = 0.5 / tr, and its critical length, Lcrit = v / 8f, the same as ¼ · tr · v. Type over the frequency to set your own, for example on input pins driven by another part.

Shortest within 10 % of the shortest critical length on the part. Route these first, as controlled impedance. Short up to 2× the shortest
